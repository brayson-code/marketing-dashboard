# Engagement

**Engagement** is your reply desk — the comments, emails, and text messages coming back at your brand, all in one place, each one click away from an AI-drafted reply. It's a tab in the **Content Lab** hub.

![the Engagement page with the YouTube comments lane open](images/engagement-1.png)

---

## What it is

Engagement gathers the conversations your content starts: top-level comments on your **YouTube** videos and **Instagram** reels, inbound **email** to your AgentMail inboxes, **SMS** from contacts via Twilio, plus your **X activity**, **LinkedIn queue**, and research **signals**. For comments, email, and texts, you don't write replies from scratch — an agent drafts one for you, and the reply only goes out after you approve it.

## Why it matters

Replies are where content turns into relationships, and they're easy to let pile up. Engagement turns "go check three platforms" into one triage pass — read, click **Draft reply**, approve, done.

---

## How to use it

Open **Content Lab → Engagement** and pick a lane with the tabs at the top:

### YouTube & Instagram comments

1. The lane lists the newest top-level comments on your connected channel/account.
2. Click **Draft reply** on a comment — an agent writes a reply in your voice (it reads your [company playbook](./concepts.md#company-playbook)).
3. The draft lands in [Approvals](./drafts.md). Approve and publish it there, and the reply posts back to the **actual comment** through your connected account.

These lanes need the platform connected on [Connections](./connections.md) — until then they show a connect prompt.

### SMS

The **SMS** lane is a two-pane chat: contacts on the left, the full thread and a reply box on the right.

- Inbound texts from your Twilio number land here automatically.
- Type a reply and click **Send** to respond directly.
- Agent-drafted texts (from automations or missions) route through [Approvals](./drafts.md) before sending.

Phone numbers are auto-normalized — `(415) 555-0123` and `4155550123` are treated the same. Needs Twilio connected; see [SMS & Text Messaging](./sms.md).

> _Screenshot: the SMS lane showing a contact list on the left and an open thread on the right._

### Daily follow-ups (CRM)

If you've turned on **daily follow-ups** from the CRM page, a banner in Engagement surfaces contacts who replied but never got a response. Once each day, an agent finds those gaps and **drafts a reply for each one** — nothing sends automatically. The drafts land in [Approvals](./drafts.md) for your review. Approve the ones that make sense; skip or edit the rest.

### Email

Inbound email to your [AgentMail](./connections.md) inboxes appears here. Click **Draft reply** and an agent writes a response; you can approve and send it **without leaving the page** — the reply goes out threaded, through your own AgentMail account.

### X Activity, LinkedIn Queue & Signals

Running lists of your X engagements, queued LinkedIn comments, and the research signals your agents have surfaced — useful for a quick read on what's moving.

---

## Tips

- **Triage daily, in one pass.** Newest comments first; draft replies as you read, then clear them all in one Approvals sweep.
- **The drafts are yours to edit.** An AI draft is a starting point — tweak it in Approvals before you approve if the moment calls for a personal touch.
- **Nothing posts on its own.** Every reply routes through [Approvals](./drafts.md) (or your explicit send click on email and SMS), same as everything else agents produce.
- **Turn on daily follow-ups once your CRM has contacts.** It's the lowest-effort way to stay on top of warm leads who fell through the cracks.
