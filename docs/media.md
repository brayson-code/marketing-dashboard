# Media Library

**Media library** is where you drop your own footage — a-roll, b-roll clips, and images — so your agents and the Hyperframes editor build reels from *your* content, not stock. Everything you upload lives in one place and stays available across every storyboard.

![the Media library with uploaded clips in a thumbnail grid](images/media-1.png)

---

## What it is

Media is a tab in the **Content** hub. Upload videos and images once and they become your workspace's library. When you lay out scenes in the [Hyperframes](./hyperframes.md) editor, the scene picker pulls from this same library — and so do your agents when they build a reel. There's no stock footage: it's your clips only.

## Why it matters

A reel is only as good as the footage behind it. Keeping your a-roll, b-roll, and brand assets in one library means every storyboard you generate already has the right shots to draw from — no re-uploading, no hunting for files.

---

## How to use it

### Upload footage

1. Open **Media** from the Content tabs.
2. **Drag clips onto the drop box**, or click it to pick files from your computer. You can upload several at once.
3. Supported: **MP4**, **MOV**, and images. The box shows progress while files upload, then refreshes your library.

### Browse and filter

- Clips and images appear as **9:16 thumbnails**, newest first, each labelled with its name and file size.
- Use the **All / Clips / Images** tabs to narrow the grid to just videos or just images.

### Delete a clip

Hover any thumbnail and click the **trash** icon to remove it from your library. You'll be asked to confirm — deleting a clip removes it everywhere it was available.

---

## Tips

- **Build your library first.** Drop in your go-to b-roll and brand assets before you start generating storyboards, so the scene editor has something to reach for.
- **Name files before uploading.** The filename is what you'll see in the grid and the scene picker — descriptive names make clips easier to find.
- **Same library, two places.** Whatever you upload here also shows up under **Your clips** in the [Hyperframes](./hyperframes.md) scene editor.
