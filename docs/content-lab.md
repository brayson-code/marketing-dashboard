# Content Lab

The **Content Lab** is where competitive intel becomes your content. It has three tools: a **Trend Radar** that shows what's hot, an **Optimize my reel** scanner that tears down *your own* reels, and a **Trial Reel Generator** that spins up fresh concepts you can turn into scripts.

![the Content Lab page with Trend Radar and idea cards](images/content-lab-1.png)

---

## What it is

Content Lab reads everything you've analyzed in [Competitor Reel Intel](./competitors.md) and helps you act on it: spot the patterns, score and improve your own reels, and generate new ideas. It's the ideation engine of your content workflow.

## Why it matters

Knowing what works is only half the battle — Content Lab closes the loop by turning insight into concrete next reels and improvements, so you always have something to make.

---

## 1. Trend Radar

The Trend Radar aggregates everything across your analyzed competitor reels into a quick read on what's working *right now*.

It shows:

- **What's hot** — the top content tags as colored chips, with a count of how many reels each appears in.
- **Top reels** — a clickable strip of the strongest reels (with view counts) you can open on Instagram.
- **What the Analyst is noticing** — a running "pulse" from the reel analyst, calling out recurring winning hooks, sounds, and structures.

**How to use it:** Just read it. The radar lights up automatically once you've analyzed a few competitor reels. Use it to decide what *kind* of reel to make next, then jump to the generator below.

![the Trend Radar with hot tags and top reels](images/content-lab-2.png)

> **Empty radar?** Analyze a handful of competitor reels first ([Competitors](./competitors.md)) — that's the data the radar reads from.

## 2. Optimize my reel

This is a full teardown of one of **your own** reels — scores, weak points, and concrete rewrites tied to the goal you pick.

**How to use it:**

1. In the **Optimize my reel** section, paste a link to one of your own reels.
2. Pick a **goal**: more views, better retention, more sales, more engagement, or general improvement.
3. Click **Scan**.

The scanner pulls your reel (and, where available, your real Instagram insights like reach and average watch time), transcribes it, and produces a report with:

- **Five scores** — Voice Impact, Visual Pull, Cognitive Grip, Emotional Hit, and Memorability.
- **Timestamped weak points** — exactly where the reel loses people.
- **Rewrites** — for each weak spot, a "Currently → Try this → Expected impact" suggestion, tailored to the goal you chose.

Recent scans appear as a row of thumbnails you can click to revisit. If a scan fails, there's a **Retry**.

![the "Optimize my reel" report with the five scores and rewrites](images/content-lab-3.png)

> **Tip:** Connecting your **Instagram** account ([Connections](./connections.md)) lets the scanner use your real performance numbers as ground truth, which makes the report far more accurate. Connecting **Deepgram** ensures the audio gets transcribed even when there's no subtitle track.

## 3. Trial Reel Generator

Generates a batch of trial reel **concepts** from current trends and competitor wins — ideas you can keep, dismiss, and turn into scripts.

**How to use it:**

1. (Optional) Type a **focus** to steer the batch — e.g. "founder POV hooks" or "product demos." Leave it blank for a general batch.
2. Click **Generate ideas**.
3. Review the concept cards. Each shows a **hook**, an **angle**, a **format**, and the **rationale** for why it should work.
4. For each idea:
   - **Keep** the ones you like (they move to the top).
   - **Dismiss** the ones you don't (they sink to the bottom).
   - On a kept idea, click **Write script** to turn it into a draft script.

Scripts you generate this way show up in [Script Studio](./script-studio.md), ready to edit and record.

## The Content Lab workflow

The three tools are designed to flow into each other:

1. **Trend Radar** tells you what's hot.
2. **Optimize my reel** shows you how your existing reels measure up and how to fix them.
3. **Trial Reel Generator** spins up new concepts based on those trends.
4. **Write script** sends a kept idea to **Script Studio**.
5. You edit, run the teleprompter, and record.

---

## Tips

- **Feed the radar first.** Content Lab is only as good as the competitor reels behind it — keep an active watchlist running.
- **Use the goal selector deliberately** in "Optimize my reel." A retention-focused scan and a sales-focused scan give very different rewrites.
- **Generate in small batches and curate hard.** Keep only the 2–3 concepts that genuinely excite you; dismiss the rest so your script queue stays sharp.
- **"Write script" is the bridge.** A kept idea isn't content until it's a script — push the winners through to Script Studio.
