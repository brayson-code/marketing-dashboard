# Analytics

**Analytics** is the performance read across everything you've connected — your website, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook Ads, and TikTok — scoped to a time range you pick. Because the connections are your own accounts, these are your *real* numbers.

![the Analytics page with the social roll-up and per-platform panels](images/analytics-1.png)

---

## What it is

One page, top to bottom:

- **Social roll-up** — the cross-channel picture first: followers, engagement, and trend lines across everything connected, so you see the whole before drilling into a platform.
- **Website, X & LinkedIn** — traffic and per-platform engagement panels.
- **YouTube** — your channel at a glance: subscriber and view metrics for the last 30 days plus your recent uploads.
- **Instagram** — your account's reach and recent media performance.
- **Facebook Ads** — spend, impressions, CTR, CPM, frequency, and ROAS from your ad account.
- **TikTok** — followers, video views, and recent video performance.

Each per-platform panel lights up once that platform is connected on [Connections](./connections.md); until then it shows a connect prompt instead of empty charts.

## Why it matters

Your agents make content and outreach decisions — this page is where you (and they) see whether it's working, without hopping between five native dashboards.

---

## How to use it

1. Open **Analytics** from the left navigation (under **Insights**).
2. Pick a **time range** at the top — 7, 30, or 90 days. The panels re-scope to match.
3. Start at the **social roll-up** for the trend, then scroll into the platform you're investigating.

---

## Tips

- **Connect before you read.** A panel is only as good as its connection — link the platform on [Connections](./connections.md) and the real numbers flow in.
- **Use it alongside Content Lab.** Analytics tells you *what happened*; the [Content Lab](./content-lab.md) "Optimize my reel" scan tells you *why* and what to change.
- **Cost lives elsewhere.** What your *agents* cost is on [Usage & Spend](./usage.md); Analytics is about how your *content and channels* perform.
