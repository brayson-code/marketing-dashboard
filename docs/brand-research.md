# Brand & Web Research

**Brand & Web Research** lets your agents look at any public website and pull back a clean read of it — the brand's colours, fonts, and logo, the page's text, and the key brand details like name, tagline, and social links. Point KeyPlayer at a URL and it captures a brand profile in seconds.

---

## What it is

Once you've connected **Firecrawl** (a research service you add under [Connections](./connections.md)), KeyPlayer and your specialist agents gain a brand-scrape ability. Give them a public web address and they fetch:

- **A brand profile** — the brand's colours, fonts, and logo.
- **A clean text capture** of the page — the readable content, stripped of the clutter.
- **Structured brand details** — the brand's name, tagline, description, and social links.

It reads **public pages only** — the same things any visitor to the site can see.

## Why it matters

So much of good marketing work starts with *understanding a brand fast* — your new client's, or a competitor's. Doing that by hand means clicking around a site, eyedropping colours, hunting for the logo, and copying text into a doc.

Brand & Web Research collapses that into a single ask. Your agents capture the look and the language in one pass, so they can onboard a new client quickly, run a competitive teardown, or match a brand's style and voice when they write content for you.

---

## How to use it

### 1. Connect Firecrawl

Brand & Web Research needs a **Firecrawl** key. Connect it once on the [Connections page](./connections.md) (under **API keys & other services**): paste your key and **Save**. That single connection turns the brand-scrape ability on for your whole team.

### 2. Ask KeyPlayer to research a site

Head to the [Boardroom](./boardroom.md) and just describe what you want, for example:

- *"Pull the brand profile for example.com — colours, fonts, and logo."*
- *"Research acme.io and tell me their tagline and social links."*
- *"Capture the homepage text for this site so we can study their messaging."*

KeyPlayer fetches the page, reads it, and replies with what it found.

### 3. Let agents use it during content work

You don't always have to ask. When a specialist is onboarding a new client or writing content meant to match a brand, it can reach for brand research on its own — pulling the brand's colours, fonts, and voice so the work it produces actually looks and sounds like that brand.

---

## What you get back

A brand research pass returns three things:

1. **The brand profile** — colours, fonts, and the logo. This is what you use to match a brand's *look*.
2. **A clean text capture** — the page's readable content, ready to quote, summarise, or study.
3. **Structured brand details** — name, tagline, description, and social links, pulled out as tidy fields rather than buried in the page.

---

## Tips

- **Use it the moment you onboard a client.** Point it at their website and capture their brand — colours, fonts, logo, voice — before you write a single post. It's the fastest way to get your agents speaking in their style.
- **Run it on a competitor.** A quick brand profile of a rival's site is a great companion to a [Competitor Reel Intel](./competitors.md) teardown — you see their look *and* their messaging side by side.
- **Match the voice, not just the colours.** The clean text capture is gold for studying *how* a brand talks. Feed it into your content work so drafts land in the right tone.
- **It reads public pages only.** Brand & Web Research sees what any visitor sees — it can't reach anything behind a login.
