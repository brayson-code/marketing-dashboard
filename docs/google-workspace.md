# Google Workspace

One connection gives your agents access to the full Google suite: Docs, Sheets, Drive, Gmail, Calendar, and Meet. You don't need to connect them one by one — a single **Connect Google** on the Connections page covers all six.

> _Screenshot: the Connections page showing the "Connect Google" tile in the connected state._

---

## What your agents can do

Once you connect Google and turn on actions (see below), your agents can:

| Capability | What happens |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Google Docs** | Create new documents and edit existing ones — drafting reports, summaries, or any long-form output directly in your Drive |
| **Google Sheets** | Build and populate spreadsheets — campaign trackers, research tables, data exports |
| **Google Drive** | Organize files into folders, move assets, keep your Drive tidy as work accumulates |
| **Gmail** | Read your inbox, draft replies, and send email through your Google account |
| **Google Calendar** | List upcoming events, create new events, and book time without leaving KeyCommand |
| **Google Meet** | Generate a Meet link for a new meeting so you can share it immediately |
| **Meeting transcripts** | Fetch the auto-transcript from a recent Meet session so an agent can summarize, extract action items, or use it in research |

Every write action — creating a Doc, sending an email, adding a Calendar event, organizing a Drive folder — is recorded in your **audit log** so you always have a trail of what agents did.

---

## How to connect

1. Open **Connections** from the left navigation.
2. Find the **Connect Google** tile in the Social accounts or integrations section.
3. Click **Connect** and complete Google's sign-in and permission consent screen. One consent covers Drive, Docs, Sheets, Gmail, Calendar, and Meet — you're done after a single pop-up.
4. The tile flips to **connected**.

---

## Turning on Google Workspace actions

Connecting Google on its own doesn't immediately let agents take write actions. You also need to enable the toggle in Settings.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Find **Google Workspace actions** and flip it **on**. (It's off by default, so no writes happen until you're ready.)

With both steps done — connected *and* the toggle on — your agents can start using the full suite above.

> If you connect Google but leave the toggle off, agents can still reference the connection for identity and sign-in purposes, but they won't create or send anything in your Google account.

---

## Good to know

- **It's one consent, not six.** You don't need to approve Drive separately from Gmail separately from Calendar — the single Google OAuth screen covers everything.
- **Every write is audit-logged.** Check the audit trail anytime if you want to see exactly what was created, moved, or sent.
- **You can disconnect anytime.** Removing the connection on the Connections page stops all Google actions immediately. The toggle in Settings is a lighter option if you just want to pause actions without fully disconnecting.
- **Agents draft before acting.** Write actions still follow your normal approval rules — if your autonomy setting routes outputs through [Approvals](./drafts.md), Google actions go there too.
