# Your Data

Everything KeyCommand creates belongs to you. You can export your entire workspace at any time with one click — no support ticket, no waiting, no questions asked.

---

## What gets exported

The **Export your data** button in Settings downloads a ZIP file containing everything your workspace has produced:

- **Documents** — any text assets, reports, or content your agents generated
- **Custom agents** — your Agent Studio definitions and playbooks
- **Knowledge graph** — the structured information your agents have built up about your business and niche
- **Automations** — your scheduled jobs (cron) and mission templates
- **CRM** — your contacts, conversation history, and follow-up records
- **History** — your conversation logs and completed task records

Nothing is left out. If KeyCommand created it, stored it, or learned it on your behalf, it's in the export.

---

## How to export

1. Open **Settings** from the left navigation.
2. Find the **Export your data** section.
3. Click the **Export** button.
4. Your browser downloads a ZIP file. The file is self-contained — open it in any standard archive tool to read the contents.

> _Screenshot: the Export your data section in Settings with the Export button._

The export is a point-in-time snapshot of your workspace. If you want an up-to-date copy, just run it again.

---

## Good to know

- **It's your data, always.** KeyCommand's bring-your-own-keys model means your data has never lived on someone else's AI account. The export just gives you a local copy to keep.
- **No lock-in.** You can take your export and use it however you want — move workspaces, switch tools, archive it, or hand it to your team.
- **Exports don't delete anything.** Downloading your data doesn't affect your workspace. Everything stays in KeyCommand exactly as it was.
