# Overview / Dashboard

The **Overview** is your home page — the first thing you see after signing in. It's a single scrollable view that answers "what's happening, what needs me, and what should I look at next?" at a glance.

![the full Overview dashboard](images/overview-1.png)

---

## What it is

The Overview is a live dashboard that updates on its own (agent cards refresh every few seconds). It's organized top-to-bottom into focal regions, and most of it reacts to the **lens** you pick at the top.

## Why it matters

Instead of hunting through pages, the Overview surfaces the few things that actually need your attention — decisions waiting in the queue, progress toward your goals, and a pulse on what your competitors and content engine are doing.

---

## The pieces, top to bottom

### Lens tabs
At the very top, the lens tabs switch which slice of your team and metrics you're viewing — **Leadership**, **Marketing**, **Revenue**, and **Operations**. Everything below the tabs (the status strip, the agent cards, the operator queue) re-filters to match the lens you choose.

### Status strip
A row of key metric cells for the active lens — a quick read on how things are trending.

### 72-hour activation card
Early on, a countdown card guides you through your first wins. It auto-hides once you've shipped your first published draft or completed the setup milestones, so it won't clutter your view forever.

### Goals & top priorities
Your **North Star** goal plus your top priorities. Each card links straight to the relevant plan in [Goals](./goals-and-missions.md), so a click takes you to the detail.

### Hero agent cards
A row of cards, one per agent on the active team. Each shows the agent's name, role, recent activity, and a live "heartbeat" so you can see who's working. Click any card to open that agent's detail page. See [Agents & Agent Studio](./agents.md).

![the row of hero agent cards with live heartbeats](images/overview-2.png)

### The Workbench row
Three widgets share this row:

- **Operator Queue** — items waiting for your decision (approvals and the like), filtered to the active lens.
- **Today's Priorities** — what the team is focused on today.
- **Weekly Snapshot** — a short rollup of the week.

### Competitor & Content Lab cards
A two-card row that ties the Overview to your content engine:

- **Competitor Intel** card — a preview of what your tracked competitors are doing. Opens [Competitors](./competitors.md).
- **Content Lab** card — a preview of what to make next. Opens [Content Lab](./content-lab.md).

### Automation Flow
A view of your active campaign's waves of agent work, if one is running. See [Goals & Missions](./goals-and-missions.md).

### Usage / spend
A full-width strip showing your Claude API spend so cost stays visible without leaving home. The full breakdown lives on the [Usage page](./usage.md).

### Knowledge map
At the very bottom, a compact graph of the facts and relationships your agents have accumulated over time. It hides itself when there's nothing to show yet.

---

## How to use it

1. **Pick a lens** at the top to focus on the part of the business you care about right now.
2. **Scan the Operator Queue** for anything waiting on you, and clear it.
3. **Glance at your goals** to keep the team's work anchored to outcomes.
4. **Click an agent card** to dig into what a specific agent has been doing.
5. **Use the Competitor and Content Lab cards** as jumping-off points into your content workflow.

---

## Tips

- The Overview **updates itself** — you don't need to refresh. If an agent is mid-task, its card glows and shows a fresh "now · …" heartbeat.
- The activation card is the fastest checklist for a brand-new workspace. Knock out its steps to light up the rest of the dashboard.
- If the **Knowledge map** is empty, that's normal early on — it fills in as agents do research and remember facts.
- Treat the **Operator Queue** as your daily to-do: it's the shortest path to the handful of decisions only you can make.
