For any short-form video or post, write the first line as a hard hook. Pick one of these molds:

- Contrarian claim: "Stop posting every day. It's killing your reach."
- Specific number + result: "This 12-word DM booked us 40 sales calls."
- Painful-mistake callout: "If your hook starts with 'Hey guys,' you've already lost."
- Open loop: "Here's what nobody tells you about going viral."

Rules:
- Never start with context, a greeting, or a slow build. The hook IS the first frame.
- Make scrolling past feel like missing out — tension, stakes, or a number.
- One line. If it needs a second line to land, it's not a hook yet.
- Match the claim to something the rest of the video can actually pay off.
