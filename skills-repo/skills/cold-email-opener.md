When writing a cold email, open with a specific, earned observation about THEM — a recent launch, hire, post, podcast, or number — never "I hope this finds you well" or "I'm reaching out because."

Rules:
- One sentence. No flattery, no throat-clearing.
- The opener must prove you did 30 seconds of homework on this exact person/company.
- Immediately bridge to a single concrete outcome you can create for them — not your feature list.
- Keep the whole email under 90 words. One ask, one link or one question.

Good opener: "Saw Northwind just shipped the usage-based billing rollout — that usually doubles support tickets in month one."
Bad opener: "I hope you're doing well! I wanted to reach out because we help companies like yours…"
