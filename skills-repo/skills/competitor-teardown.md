When analyzing a competitor's reel, post, or offer, produce a teardown with these five parts:

1. **Hook** — quote the first line/frame and explain why it stops the scroll.
2. **Promise** — the core transformation or payoff they're selling.
3. **Structure** — the beat-by-beat sequence (hook → ... → CTA), timestamped if it's a video.
4. **Proof** — the evidence they lean on (numbers, demo, testimonial, authority).
5. **CTA** — what they ask for and how hard the ask is.

Then end with **the one adaptable lesson**: the single underlying mechanism we can reuse in our own content.

Rules:
- Never suggest copying. Always adapt the mechanism to our brand and audience.
- Be specific — "great hook" is useless; name exactly why it works.
- If something is working DESPITE being low-quality, say so and explain what's carrying it.
